How to play

  1. Create or join: Type your name (or pick a character). Create a room or join with a code.
  2. Players vs spectators: Players buzz and guess. Spectators can watch and chat (no playing).
  3. Buzz once per frame: You get one guess per frame — no spam. Buzz early if you’re confident.
  4. Rounds & frames: The host runs the game. Frames reveal gradually for each round.
  5. Room lock: Hosts can lock the room to stop late players. Spectators may still join.
  6. Upload frames: Use Upload frames to submit a movie frame. It’s reviewed before going live.

⚠ Tip: If anything glitches, just reload — your session will reconnect instantly.
